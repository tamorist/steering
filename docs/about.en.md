# About this site

**Steering Within Limits** brings together three proposals for a civilizational transition, the reasoning behind them, and the tools to explore them.

## Status

Everything here is a **working draft**. All names, ratios, and figures are illustrative. They show a direction, not a final design, and are meant to be rewritten through expert review, civic deliberation, and pilots.

## Languages

English is the master text. The Japanese pages are translations; in case of any discrepancy, the English version prevails. (The Japanese series 「地球のOSを書き換える」 is an adaptation for Japanese readers and includes a Japan-specific estimate.)

## Facts and figures

Facts and statistics were checked in October 2026 against public sources (UN, IMF, IPCC, Global Carbon Project, NOAA, FAO, World Inequality Report, and others). Approximate figures are rounded. If you find an error, please let us know.

## How AI was used

These proposals were written by one individual, with extensive use of generative AI for research, structure, drafting, figures, and the simulator code. The author decided what to write and what to adopt, and takes responsibility for all of it.

This is also a small example of what the proposals argue for. Drawing up an institutional blueprint used to require a large organization, such as a research institute or a government. AI makes it possible for one citizen to produce a working draft and publish it in a form anyone can check. The proposals follow the principle that "AI supports, but does not decide." So does this site: AI helped build it, but what is desirable is for the people who read, test, and deliberate on it to decide.

So please judge these proposals on whether they work as a blueprint. Facts and figures have been checked against public sources, but errors surely remain. If you find one, or have a better idea, please let us know.
