# Steering Within Limits
### Three proposals for a civilizational transition — one-page overview


!!! info "About this page"
    The key points in about 3 minutes. For the whole idea with the reasoning behind it, see Start here [→](start.md) (about 15 minutes).

**The problem.** Decades of effort on climate and other global risks have not bent the curves. The cause is not a lack of effort, goodwill, or technology. Our civilization's operating system, an economy built to expand and a politics built around nation-states, has reached limits it was never designed for. Efficiency gains are swallowed by overcompetition, benefits flow upward, and planetary boundaries are being crossed. Everyone acting rationally produces a collective failure.

**Why now.** A civilization that grew without steering must learn to **steer within limits**: strict planetary guardrails, freedom within them, continual data-driven correction, and goals set democratically. This is neither central planning nor market fundamentalism. The tools to do it now exist: planetary data, digital payments, AI translation, and citizens' assemblies. Crises will come, and the speed of the response will depend on whether a working alternative is ready.

**Three gaps, three proposals.**

| | Gap | Proposal | In one line |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A** | No actor speaks for the future across borders | **Youth GCC** | A youth-led global citizens' congress with a World Citizens' Assembly, a World Policy Institute, and a World Policy Laboratory |
| **B** | No channel from scattered civic will to decisions | **Global Citizen Will Platform** | A federation of existing movements that makes support visible and connects it to governments, courts, elections, and markets |
| **C** | No blueprint for what comes next | **Planetary OS** | Operating rules under which reducing pays: a one-way cap, 90% of revenue returned to citizens, a share of historical liability, and a club worth joining |

**How they fit.** Youth GCC launches with Planetary OS as its first agenda. Its World Citizens' Assembly reviews the Standard; its World Policy Institute drafts it; its World Policy Laboratory runs pilots. The platform carries the Citizen Dividend Simulator and one question to politics: not "please act," but **"will you connect to this?"** A blueprint without carriers stays on paper; carriers without a blueprint lose direction; both need a channel to reach decisions.

**Principles.** Complement, do not replace, states and the UN. Legitimacy is earned through open participation and independent evaluation, not declared. Experts inform; citizens decide values; AI supports but never decides. Every design is a working draft.

**First 18 months (Stage 1).** The first edition of the Planetary OS Standard; a public Citizen Dividend Simulator; the launch of Youth GCC with co-founders and partner movements; a first citizens' assembly review; talks with pilot hosts.
