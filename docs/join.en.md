# Join as a Co-Initiator

## Help build the Youth GCC, and its first agenda: a world where reducing pays



---

### In short

- **The situation:** decades of effort have not bent the curves of the climate crisis. Not for lack of goodwill, but because there is no actor for the future across borders, no channel from civic will to decisions, and no concrete blueprint for what comes next.
- **What we are building:** a package of three proposals. **Youth GCC** (who acts), the **Global Citizen Will Platform** (how civic will is connected to decisions), and **Planetary OS** (a policy blueprint under which reducing pays).
- **What we are asking:** not to sign a petition, but to **join as a Co-Initiator**: to shape, test, and co-own this work from the beginning.

---

## 1. Why we are reaching out now

For years, millions of young people have taken to the streets. Their efforts have changed the conversation, but they remain scattered across countries and campaigns, and they rarely reach the point where decisions are made.

We think two things can change that:

- **A concrete question.** Instead of asking leaders to "do more," we can show them a working design and ask: **"Will you connect to this?"** Our first agenda is **Planetary OS**: a one-way cap on fossil fuels, with most of the revenue returned directly to citizens, so that most households come out ahead.
- **A shared channel.** Instead of each movement working alone, we can connect our efforts through an open platform, while every organization keeps its own name and campaigns.

This is not meant to be one organization's project. It is designed to be **shaped by, and belong to, the wider movement** from day one. That is why we are looking for Co-Initiators now, while the design is still open.

---

## 2. What being a Co-Initiator means

| You can | In practice |
|---|---|
| **Shape the design** | Review and comment on the package and on Proposals A, B, and C before wider release |
| **Test it with your community** | Use the Citizen Dividend Simulator and the Planetary OS materials in your own campaigns, and tell us what works |
| **Co-own the result** | Be recognized as a co-founding partner. Outputs are published as a shared standard, not under any single name |
| **Keep your identity** | Your organization keeps its own name, priorities, and campaigns. Joining does not mean merging |

Being a Co-Initiator carries no financial obligation and no exclusive commitment. You decide how much you want to take part.

---

## 3. Who we are looking for

- 🌐 **Youth-led organizations and climate movements:** groups that want their local work to connect to a global effort
- 🛠️ **Civic tech developers and platforms:** people who can help build deliberation tools, the support dashboard, and the simulator
- 🔬 **Researchers and experts:** in economics, climate science, law, and deliberative democracy, to review and co-author the Planetary OS Standard
- 🕊️ **Experienced leaders, Indigenous and faith leaders:** to serve on the advisory layer, offering advice and experience while young people make the decisions

Individuals are welcome as well as organizations.
